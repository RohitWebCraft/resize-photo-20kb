How to Use FormFit

FormFit helps you easily resize your passport photo and signature to 20KB or 50KB for MP Online and other government job applications, SSC, UPSC, banking, railway and state exam forms. No registration is needed.

Resize photo to 20KB, 50KB or 100KB

Open the Image Resizer tab, upload your JPG or PNG, and tap Under 20KB, Under 50KB or Under 100KB. The photo compress for exam process runs instantly, and you can see the original and new size in KB or MB before you download.

Signature resizer

Scan or photograph your signature on white paper, upload it, and choose the size your exam form asks for. The signature resizer keeps the text sharp while reducing file size.

Image to PDF converter online

Upload certificates, marksheets or ID proofs in the Image to PDF tab, arrange the order, remove any you don't need, and download one combined PDF ready for upload.

Compress PDF

Use the PDF Compressor tab to reduce a PDF before uploading it to a government portal. Always check the size limit in your form's instructions.

Private and offline

FormFit works completely in your browser. Your photos and documents are never uploaded to any server, so the tool keeps working even with a weak connection once the page has loaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize a photo to 20KB online?

Open the Image Resizer tab, upload your JPG or PNG photo and tap “Under 20KB”. FormFit lowers the quality and, if needed, the dimensions until the file is below 20KB. Then tap Download. No login or app is needed.

How can I compress my signature to 50KB for an exam form?

Scan or photograph your signature on plain white paper, upload it in the Image Resizer and tap “Under 50KB”. If your form asks for a different limit, type your own size in KB or MB in the Custom size box.

What photo and signature size do government exam forms need?

Every recruitment form has its own rules for file size, width, height and format. Always read the notification or the upload instructions on your form, then set the same limits in FormFit, for example 20KB, 50KB or 100KB.

Can I set an exact width and height in pixels?

Yes. Use the Width and Height boxes in the Image Resizer. Keep “Keep aspect ratio” ticked to avoid stretching the photo, or untick it to set both values freely. You can also tap “Resize only” to change the dimensions without any KB limit.

Are my photos and documents uploaded to a server?

No. FormFit works completely inside your browser. Your photos, signatures and PDFs stay on your phone or computer and are never sent to any server.

How do I convert multiple images into one PDF?

Open the Image to PDF tab, select all your images, arrange the page order with the arrow buttons, remove any you do not need, and tap “Download Combined PDF”. Each image becomes one page.

Why does my PDF not get smaller in the PDF Compressor?

The compressor shrinks the JPEG images inside a PDF, so it works best on scanned or photo-based PDFs. Text-only PDFs are already small, and some PDF types (for example password-protected files) cannot be compressed in the browser.

Can I use FormFit offline or on my mobile phone?

Yes. The site is mobile friendly and all tools run in your browser. After you open it once, it can also work without internet, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

Is FormFit free? Do I need to register?

FormFit is free to use. There is no login, no registration and no watermark.

Which image format do I get after resizing?

Resized images are saved as JPG, which is the format most exam and government portals accept. PNG photos are converted to JPG automatically.
