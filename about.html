About FormFit

FormFit is a free set of online tools made for students and job applicants who need to upload photos, signatures and documents to exam and government forms. Most portals have strict file size limits such as 20KB, 50KB or 100KB, and FormFit helps you meet them quickly.

What you can do

Resize a photo or signature to a target size, convert several images into one PDF, compress a PDF, add a name and date strip to a photo, and join a photo with a signature.

Private by design

All tools run inside your browser on your own device. Your files are not uploaded to any server, there is no login and no registration.

Please note

Every form has its own rules for size, dimensions and format. Always check the instructions on your application before uploading. Contact us with suggestions or problems.
