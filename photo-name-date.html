Add Name and Date to Photo Online for Exam Forms

Many exam and government job forms ask for a passport photo with your name and date written below it. Upload your photo, type your name, pick the date and download. Free, no login, and your photo never leaves your device.

How to add name and date to your photo

Upload your passport size photo, enter your name exactly as on your application, choose the date and tap the button. FormFit adds a white strip with your name and date below the photo. If your form has a size limit, type it in KB or MB (for example 50 KB) and the image is compressed to fit. Need only a smaller file? Use the photo resizer to 20KB, 50KB or 100KB. To submit a photo and signature together, try join photo and signature.
