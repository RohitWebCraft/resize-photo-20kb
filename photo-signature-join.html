How to combine photo and signature

Upload your photo and your signature, pick stacked or side-by-side layout, and tap the button. Both pictures are placed on a clean white background. Add a maximum size in KB or MB if your form has a limit. If the form needs them as separate files, use the photo and signature resizer to 20KB or 50KB instead. To add your name and date under the photo, see add name and date to photo.
